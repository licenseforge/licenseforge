# NIST SP 800-53 Rev 5 control matrix

Human-readable view of [`oscal/component-definition.json`](oscal/component-definition.json): the controls of the Moderate-baseline families that the LicenseForge server implements, partly implements, plans, or leaves to the deploying organization. This is not the whole Moderate baseline. Controls that are purely organizational (PS, PE, AT, most of IR and PL) are the operator's and aren't listed. Status is as of `main` on 2026-11-04.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| implemented | In `main`, with the tests cited as evidence |
| partial | In `main`, with the gaps listed |
| planned | A Roadmap item, not in `main` |
| inherited | Met by the deploying organization or its platform (IdP, Kubernetes, PostgreSQL); the product supports it |

**Delegated to the identity provider** (ADR-0003). LicenseForge stores no passwords, so it has no password policy, lockout or recovery of its own: AC-7, IA-5(1) and IA-12 are met by the operator's OIDC provider. LicenseForge records the assurance the IdP asserts (amr/acr) and lets owners set a per-tenant MFA policy; per-request enforcement follows (ADR-0038; IA-2(1), IA-2(2), IA-2(6)).

Keep this file and the OSCAL file in step; `compliance/compliance_test.go` fails if their statuses differ or a cited file or test is gone.
